About the data

Market data comes from the public OKX REST API, refreshed approximately every 30 seconds while this page is open. Selected coins are the 50 highest-24h-USDT-turnover active USDT spot pairs returned by OKX; this is not a global top-50-by-market-cap ranking.

All four portfolios maintain separate virtual accounts in local browser storage. A page refresh retains the last local ledger, but other devices do not share it. The bot only evaluates trades while the page is open. Orders are simulated at quoted ask/bid with estimated fees and slippage, not exchange fills. If OKX blocks browser requests, the page explicitly shows an unavailable feed.

No guaranteed profits; this experimental strategy has not passed a long-duration out-of-sample backtest.
