Strategy & risk engine

3× perpetual trend confirmation (experimental)

Long when both 24-hour and 1-hour momentum are positive; short when both are negative. Indicative take-profit is 2% in the favorable underlying-price direction, and stop-loss is 1% against the position. These are illustrative trade rules, not price predictions. At 3× leverage, a 1% price move is approximately a 3% margin change before costs.

Entry filter: absolute 24h move 1–8%, same-direction 1h change 0.3–2.5%, 24h turnover above $1m, spread below 0.25%. One position per swap; 5% daily portfolio loss halts new entries. Simplified isolated-margin liquidation approximation; no real exchange liquidation engine.

Model assumption: 0.10% taker fee and 0.05% slippage per side on full 3× notional, plus bid/ask spread. Funding is not accrued until reliable funding data can be integrated; therefore paper returns are incomplete and optimistic or pessimistic versus real perpetual fills.

About the data

Market data comes from the public KuCoin REST API, refreshed approximately every 30 seconds while the page is open. Selected coins are the 50 highest-24h-USDT-turnover USDT-settled perpetual swaps returned by KuCoin; this is not a global top-50-by-market-cap ranking. Positions are simulated with fixed 3× notional and isolated margin.

All four portfolios maintain separate virtual accounts in local browser storage. A page refresh retains the last local ledger, but other devices do not share it. The bot only evaluates trades while the page is open. Orders are simulated using bid/ask quotes and estimated fees and slippage, not exchange fills. Funding, maintenance-margin brackets, insurance fund, mark-price liquidation and auto-deleveraging are not fully modeled. If KuCoin blocks browser requests, the page explicitly shows an unavailable feed.

No guaranteed profits; this experimental strategy has not passed a long-duration out-of-sample backtest.
